SemanticAuth Sign in

Privacy policy

Last updated 20 October 2026

SemanticAuth is a hackathon demo, not a commercial service. It runs on the Base Sepolia test network and moves test USDC with no real value.

SemanticAuth is an authorization gateway between AI agents and payments: your agent proposes a task, you approve it in the dashboard, and the gateway checks every purchase against what you approved before paying. This policy explains what data the service handles and why.

Google user data we access

When you sign in with Google we request only the openid, email and profile scopes and receive your Google account ID, email address, name and profile picture. We do not access your Gmail, Drive, contacts, calendar or any other Google data.

How we use Google user data

  • The account ID identifies your account so you can sign in again.
  • Your name, email and picture are shown to you in the dashboard.
  • We do not use it for advertising, do not sell it, do not use it to train AI models and do not share it with anyone except as described below.

SemanticAuth's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other data we collect

  • Tasks your agent proposes: the purpose, your request in your words, what may and may not be bought, and the budget.
  • The URLs your agent asks to buy and the merchants' payment offers.
  • Your decisions, purchase outcomes, spending against your allowance, and API key metadata. API keys are stored only as a hash.
  • A session cookie that keeps you signed in for up to 7 days. No tracking cookies.

Who else receives data

  • Google, to sign you in.
  • TypeSafe (Jev API), which checks each paid offer. It receives the merchant's offer text and the task it is judged against. It never receives your name or email.
  • Railway, which hosts the service and keeps standard request logs.
  • Base Sepolia, a public blockchain. Payments, their amounts and recipient addresses are visible to anyone. Your name and email are never written on chain.
  • Merchants your agent buys from receive the payment and the request.

Storage, retention and deletion

Data is stored in the service's database on Railway for the duration of the demo. The database may be reset at any time, which deletes everything. You can revoke your API key at any time in the dashboard. To have your account and all its data deleted, email czm@fel.cvut.cz; we delete it within 30 days.

Security

Traffic is encrypted with HTTPS. API keys are stored as hashes and shown only once. Agents never receive the wallet key that signs payments.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
